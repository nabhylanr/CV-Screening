# Data Analyst Intern — EduKarir (fictional company)

EduKarir is an online learning platform for career preparation. We are looking for a Data Analyst Intern (3 months, remote) to support the product and content teams with data-driven insights.

## Responsibilities
- Query and prepare learning-activity data for weekly reports.
- Build and maintain dashboards for the product and content teams.
- Analyse learner behaviour and present findings to non-technical stakeholders.

## Requirements
Must-have:
- R1. Writes SQL to query and aggregate data.
- R2. Uses Python (pandas) for data cleaning and analysis.
- R3. Has built dashboards or visualisations with Looker Studio, Tableau, or Power BI.
- R4. Applies basic statistics (descriptive statistics, hypothesis testing).

Nice-to-have:
- R5. Experience with A/B testing or experiment analysis.
- R6. Experience in education/edtech, or presenting insights to non-technical stakeholders.
